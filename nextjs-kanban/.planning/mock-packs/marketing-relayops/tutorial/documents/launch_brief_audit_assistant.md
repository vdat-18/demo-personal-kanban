# Launch Brief: Audit Assistant

## Launch Summary

RelayOps is preparing the public launch of `Audit Assistant`, a new module for mid-market operations teams that need better audit readiness across evidence collection, control ownership, and remediation tracking.

## Launch Window

- External launch week: `2026-05-04`
- Planning week: `2026-04-06` through `2026-04-10`
- Content engine window: `2026-04-20` through `2026-06-12`

## Business Goal

Create a launch-ready content engine that gives marketing, product marketing, content, and sales enablement one shared message architecture and one shared execution plan.

## Core Audience

- Primary audience: operations leaders at companies with 250-1,500 employees in regulated services, logistics, and specialty manufacturing
- Buying problem: audit prep is manual, evidence is fragmented, and accountability is unclear until a deadline is already at risk

## Primary Message

Audit Assistant helps operations teams move from reactive audit preparation to always-ready compliance operations without adding headcount.

## Primary KPI Target

Generate `45 assisted demo requests` influenced by launch content between `2026-04-20` and `2026-06-12`.

## Secondary Metrics

- 1,200 visits to launch-aligned content assets
- 180 CTA clicks from content to product demo pages
- 3 sales-ready assets that can be reused in live pipeline conversations

## Constraints

- The team can support one major asset per week plus repurposed derivatives.
- Sales needs objection-handling content before the end of week three.
- No customer case study will be ready before week six.

## Required Planning Outputs

- approved messaging matrix
- 8-week editorial calendar
- three priority content briefs
- channel distribution checklist
- KPI tracker specification and reporting cadence
