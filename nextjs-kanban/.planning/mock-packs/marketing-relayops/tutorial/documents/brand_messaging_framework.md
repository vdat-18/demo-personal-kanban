# RelayOps Brand Messaging Framework

## Company Position

RelayOps helps compliance-heavy operations teams replace spreadsheet-driven audits, ad hoc evidence chasing, and last-minute remediation scrambles with repeatable operating workflows.

## Product In Scope

- Product name: `Audit Assistant`
- Category framing: audit readiness and compliance workflow automation
- Launch theme: move from reactive audit prep to always-ready operations

## Primary Audience

- Primary buyer: Director or Senior Manager of Operations at a mid-market company with recurring customer, internal, or regulatory audits
- Secondary audiences:
  - Compliance manager who owns evidence collection
  - RevOps or systems lead who maintains proof across tools

## Approved Primary Message

Audit Assistant helps mid-market operations teams shift from scramble-based audit preparation to always-ready compliance operations without adding headcount.

## Proof Pillars

1. Evidence stays organized instead of scattered across spreadsheets, inboxes, and shared drives.
2. Readiness gaps are visible before the audit window instead of during the scramble week.
3. Teams can show progress and accountability without building custom reporting from scratch.

## Approved Language

- Use:
  - audit readiness
  - evidence collection
  - control ownership
  - operational accountability
  - always-ready
- Avoid:
  - autonomous governance
  - zero-touch compliance
  - guaranteed audit success
  - instant certification

## Tone Guidance

- Clear and operational
- Credible rather than visionary
- Focused on reducing scramble, not replacing people

## Content Implications

- Content should connect operational pain to measurable readiness outcomes.
- Messaging should address both workflow efficiency and leadership visibility.
- SEO content should ladder up to the approved primary message rather than drift into generic compliance terminology.
