# Common Sales Objections

## Objection 1

### What prospects say

"We already have spreadsheets and a shared drive. Why would we buy another layer?"

### What this means

The buyer does not yet believe that fragmentation and lack of ownership create measurable risk.

### Content implication

Show the operational cost of scattered evidence and duplicate requests.

## Objection 2

### What prospects say

"Our audits only get painful a few times a year."

### What this means

They frame the problem as temporary pain rather than an ongoing operating issue.

### Content implication

Position readiness as continuous operating leverage, not seasonal panic management.

## Objection 3

### What prospects say

"Compliance owns this, not operations."

### What this means

The prospect underestimates how much operational data and execution sit outside the compliance team.

### Content implication

Explain the shared workflow between operations, systems, and compliance.

## Objection 4

### What prospects say

"We cannot prove ROI on audit software."

### What this means

The buyer needs a business case linked to reduced scramble, faster reporting, and fewer repeat findings.

### Content implication

Include ROI framing and a leadership-visibility angle in at least one priority brief.
